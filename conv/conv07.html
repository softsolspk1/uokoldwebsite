The Annual Convocation 2007 of the university held on Monday, April 30, 2007 at Valika Cricket Stadium, University of Karachi. It was presided over by Prof. Dr. Pirzada Qasim Raza Siddiqui the Vice Chancellor of the university.The convocation was also attended by members of the senate and syndicate of the university, eminent citizens, faculty members, parents and graduating students.

Degrees were conferred on students who did their Bachelors, Masters and MPhil/PhD from the University in 2007.
Speaking on the occasion, Vice Chancellor emphasized upon the students to work selflessly for the progress and betterment of the country..
