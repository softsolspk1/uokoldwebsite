Be it known to all concerned that the Annual Convocation for the regular students (under semester system) of the University of Karachi is expected to be held shortly. to confer degrees on the desiring candidates who have passed Bachelors (Honours), B.S., Pharm-D, Masters and M.Pharm in 2009 & 2010. Degrees shall also be conferred on those who obtained their M.S./ M.D./ M.Phil/ Ph.D./ D.Litt./ D.Sc. in 2009, 2010 onwards.
       
Medals and Merit Certificates shall also be awarded to the position holders of various Departments (where available) in the Examinations mentioned above.

The desirous candidates of degree (who have not yet received their degrees) are advised to apply for registration to the Annual Convocation on prescribed application form available on the left.

 

 

The same are also available from the Convocation Camp Office, Silver Jubilee Gate No.1, Main University Road, University of Karachi from 30th May, 2011 on (Monday). Duly filled in forms (with required documents) shall be received only in the Convocation Camp office along with prescribed Registration and Degree forms with fees to be deposited at the bank branches at Silver Jubilee Gate after endorsement by the Camp Office during 10:00 a.m. to 01:00 p.m. and on Friday 10:00 to 12:00 Noon till 23rd July, 2011 on (Saturday).

No candidate will be allowed to enter the convocation pavillion without wearing prescribed Academic Costume/Robe.

( Prof. M. Kaleem Raza Khan )
